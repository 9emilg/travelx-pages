Program

Wednesday, 13 January 2027 · International Fair Plovdiv

  1. 09:00 – 10:00Registration & Welcome Reception
  2. 10:00 – 10:20Official OpeningOfficial opening of TRAVEL X 2027 with representatives of the Ministry of Tourism, the Municipality of Plovdiv and TRAVEL X.
  3. 10:20 – 11:00Keynote|How Destinations Become Success StoriesAn international case study exploring how a relatively small country or tourism market defines its identity, builds strong positioning and successfully strengthens its place on the global tourism map.
  4. 11:00 – 11:50Keynote|Finding a Common Voice: The Identity of a DestinationPlovdiv presents its new city branding strategy. The Municipality of Plovdiv will present its new approach to city branding as a practical example of how a clear and consistent visual and communication identity can be built at local level to strengthen tourism positioning and recognition.
  5. 11:50 – 12:40Panel 1|The European Traveller Is Changing. Are We?A data-driven look at where tourism growth in Europe is coming from and how traveller behaviour, spending, seasonality, source markets and booking patterns are changing.
  6. 12:40 – 13:30Lunch break
  7. 13:30 – 14:20Panel 2|The Data Language of a Destination: You Can’t Manage What You Don’t MeasureCan tourism be managed effectively without a common data framework? The panel will explore the lack of unified methodology, consistent data collection and integrated analysis in Bulgaria, and how these gaps limit informed decisions, market insight and the development of better tourism products.
  8. 15:10 – 16:00Panel 3|The Potential Beyond Sun & SkiWhich tourism verticals can unlock Bulgaria’s next phase of growth, attract new audiences, extend the season and strengthen the country’s appeal across international markets?
  9. 16:00 – 16:30Coffee & Networking
  10. 16:30 – 17:10Panel 4|Getting Here Is Part of the ProductHow do air, rail and road connectivity shape a region’s ability to attract international visitors, develop new markets and compete for tourism growth?
  11. 21:00 – 00:00TravelX Welcome Reception Party & Networking
  1. 10:00 – 10:50Panel 5|Places, Culture & Experiences Worth Travelling ForHow can cultural heritage, iconic landmarks, urban life and authentic local experiences turn a place from a stop along the way into a reason to travel in its own right?
  2. 10:50 – 11:30Panel 6|Bulgaria: The View From OutsideHow do tour operators, travel agents and international travel trade partners shape the way Bulgaria is presented, packaged and sold abroad? What do they need in order to position the country more effectively, reach new audiences and generate stronger demand?
  3. 11:30 – 12:20Panel 7|Can You Actually Buy Bulgaria?Bulgaria has remarkable hotels, cultural sites, tours, events, wineries and experiences. But how easy are they to discover, combine, book and pay for as part of one seamless journey?
  4. 12:20 – 13:30Lunch break
  5. 13:30 – 14:20Panel 8|AI Is Changing Travel. How Does It Affect the Industry?AI is reshaping how travellers discover, research, plan, book and experience a trip. How should tourism businesses and public organisations adapt to remain visible, relevant and competitive?
  6. 14:50 – 15:40Panel 9|Events That Make DestinationsHow can festivals, concerts, sports and cultural events become powerful reasons to travel, attract new audiences, extend the season and build long-term recognition for a place?
  7. 15:40 – 16:10Coffee & Networking
  8. 16:10 – 17:00Panel 10|More Than a Place to StayTravellers increasingly expect more than accommodation. How are hotels, resorts, SPA and wellness concepts evolving into complete experiences that drive loyalty, longer stays and higher value?
  9. 17:00 – 17:40Panel 11|Flavours That Tell a StoryHow can food, wine and local culinary traditions become a recognisable part of Bulgaria’s identity and a powerful reason to discover the country through its regions, culture and people?
  10. 19:00 – 00:00TravelX Official Cocktail & Awards
  1. 11:00 – 14:00Exclusive city tour|The Living City: A Journey Through the AgesAn immersive exploration of Plovdiv’s history with an audio guide. Pre-registration required.
